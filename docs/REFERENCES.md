# Research references and source registry — initial

This collection distinguishes **framework sources**, **project inspirations**, **candidate data sources**, and **work requiring verification**. Links are pointers for human review; inclusion is not a reuse license or assertion that data quality has been checked for every item.

## Enterprise modeling and development methods

- Lantow, B., Sandkuhl, K., & Stirna, J. (2022). *Enterprise Modeling with 4EM: Perspectives and Method*. Springer. https://research.rtu.lv/en/publications/enterprise-modeling-with-4em-perspectives-and-method/
- OMiLAB community, *4EM tooling*. https://code.omilab.org/resources/modelling_tools/4em — six traditional views and a contemporary products/services extension.
- *Supporting Early Phases of Digital Twin Development with Enterprise Modeling and Capability Management*. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7254533/ — discusses six connected 4EM submodels and stakeholder participation.
- Design Council, *Double Diamond*. https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/the-double-diamond/ — discover, define, develop, deliver, iteratively.
- Ryan Singer, *Shape Up*. https://basecamp.com/shapeup — appetite, bounded shaping, integrated slices.
- NIST, *Human-Centered Design*. https://www.nist.gov/itl/iad/human-centered-technologies/human-factors-human-centered-design — iterative evaluation and holistic usability.
- ISO 9241-210:2019, *Human-centred design for interactive systems*. https://www.iso.org/standard/77520.html
- GitHub/Open Source Guides, *Starting an Open Source Project* and *Leadership and Governance*. https://opensource.guide/

## Chess composition primary conventions

- World Federation for Chess Composition, *Codex of Chess Compositions*. https://www.wfcc.ch/rules/codex/ — authors, stipulations, solutions, soundness, publication norms.
- WFCC, *Chess Compositions*. https://www.wfcc.ch/chess-compositions/ — art and illustrative examples.
- WFCC, *Databases*. https://www.wfcc.ch/databases/ — directories of specialist collections.
- YACPDB. https://www.yacpdb.org/ — candidate composition source; verify access and redistribution terms.
- British Chess Problem Society. https://www.theproblemist.org/ — composer community and archival material.

5. **Library licenses:** Review compatibility before choosing GPL/copyleft or more permissive source distribution.
