# Experiment 001 — A composition worth opening

**Status:** shaped proposal, not implemented. **Product:** The Art of Chess Composition. **Purpose:** validate the complete read–solve–understand loop before building an archive or narrative standard.

## Reader promise

Open a carefully presented mate-in-two attributed to Paul Morphy; try the puzzle on a working board; ask for hints; understand the intended solution and why it is designed that way; see the publication and authorship uncertainty; follow a link to another work.

## Core hypothesis

A small, curated interactive interpretation is enjoyable and instructive without account registration, level tracking, AI-generated spectacle or an extensive database.

## Inputs to independently verify

- Original board position, side to move, stipulation, intended solution and defenses.
- Early publication details (1856) and attribution uncertainty, including later historiographical debate.
- Rights to any archival scan or illustration used.
- Distinguish the designed composition from a recorded competitive game.

**Do not implement chess data from prose memory or screenshots alone.** Cross-check an authoritative edition or archival record before freezing FEN and moves.

## One vertical slice

- Artifact page: title, composer attribution status, date/origin note, clear stipulation.
- Functional accessible board: click/touch and keyboard interaction; reset, try move, undo.
- Solutions: show why a legal but incorrect move does not satisfy the stipulation; at least two progressively revealing hints; reveal intended solution and relevant defenses.
- Editorial explanation: economy, quiet key or another *verified* artistic mechanism, plus short contextual note.
- Provenance: visible clickable sources, uncertainty note and credits.
- Presentation: responsive typography, restrained board palette and piece motion; sound optional and not needed.
- Tests: mechanically reproducible position and intended continuations, hint anchor validity, keyboard/reduced-motion checks.

## Explicit exclusions

Accounts, API backend, general-purpose composition search, AI tutor, leaderboard, media production pipeline, community marketplace, complex branching animation, multi-board simul and proprietary authoring format.

## Acceptance / evaluation

- Chess reviewer verifies position, stipulation, first move and defensive branches.
- A chess-curious reader can attempt, receive hints, see the solution and recover from errors without explanation from a developer.
- A reader understands that the work is a composition and its authorship isn't completely settled.
- A contributor can locate and edit the puzzle narrative without learning the internals of a large framework.
- The creator judges the result worth voluntarily reopening and can articulate shortcomings.
- All observable errors and editorial disagreements become Tickets, not hidden exceptions.

## Next tracer bullet

Replay Morphy's 1858 Opera Game as a real historical game, with one meaningful move-guessing checkpoint and source notes. Compare the two slices before extracting a shared narrative schema.
