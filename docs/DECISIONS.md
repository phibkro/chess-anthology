# Decisions, assumptions and unresolved questions

The project began as personal curiosity about a Morphy-attributed composition and grew into a two-product open chess literature concept. **This is a decision log, not a retrospective claim of validated demand.**

## Adopted intent

- **D001** Project name is Chess Anthology.
- **D002** Two distinct editorial products: Chess Through History; The Art of Chess Composition.
- **D003** Optimize for curiosity, craftsmanship, honesty, openness and agency instead of retention mechanics.
- **D004** Use historically sourced, human-composed and game-derived chess artifacts with their proper labels and creators.
- **D005** Start with a polished working vertical slice, not a business plan or broad infrastructure.
- **D006** Agent-powered source discovery and import are optional, inspectable later capabilities.
- **D007** Favor an open portable format, but design it through real authored examples rather than declaring an untested standard.

## Hypotheses to test

- **H1** A composed mate-in-two with story and graded hints is intrinsically satisfying.
- **H2** The historical-game and composition products benefit from one reader core.
- **H3** Audio and historical images improve the experience when optional and accurate.
- **H4** Meaningful source and creator attribution enriches discovery.
- **H5** Plain-text authoring plus PGN/FEN is sufficient for the first narrative format.
- **H6** A librarian-style agent could make personally useful collections without generating generic AI lessons.

## Unresolved decisions

- Chosen chess/board and frontend libraries, including dependency licenses.
- First software and content licenses.
- Specific source rights and historical details for first examples.
- Whether a game-tree engine covers the composition cases in scope.
- Stable narration-to-position anchoring representation and multimedia timing.
- The first contributor-facing content syntax.
- Whether single repository and runtime sufficiently serve both products.
- Future hosted import/community functions, if evidence supports them.
- Brand visual identity and whether "Chess Anthology" can be used publicly without conflict.

## How decisions change

Record an ADR or issue containing: context, alternatives, chosen tradeoff, supporting evidence, reversibility and affected 4EM goal/rule/requirement IDs. Close or revise hypotheses when tested. Avoid speculating about success from mockups alone.
